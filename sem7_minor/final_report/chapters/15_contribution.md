# 15. INDIVIDUAL CONTRIBUTION

This chapter states how the work of Group 148 was shared among its three members, Rohit Yadav, Vishal and Hardik Mehlawat, all students of B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering in the seventh semester. The project was carried out jointly. By the team's own decision, the review was not divided into personal assignments, and no member was made solely responsible for any part of it.

Instead, each of the three took part in every work package listed in Table 6.2: searching for and screening the literature, reading the primary studies in full and extracting their data, building the taxonomy and carrying out the comparative and gap analysis, writing the text together with its tables and figures, verifying the references and checking the records for consistency, and preparing the project documentation, from the weekly progress reports to this final report. The weekly progress reports were likewise submitted for the group as a whole, each listing all three members. The main decisions about the review were taken by the team together, under the guide's direction, rather than by one member acting alone.
