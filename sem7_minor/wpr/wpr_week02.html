Literature collection round 1 was completed following the Week 1 search protocol. Using the defined query seeds, candidate papers were gathered in six topic strands (GAN-era CNN detection, diffusion-model detection, frequency-domain forensics, transformer/CLIP-based detection, datasets and surveys, explainability and background) from arXiv, publisher and proceedings pages, and Google Scholar.
